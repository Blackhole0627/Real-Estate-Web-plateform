Marriott bets on Santo Domingo with the Caribbean's first Executive Apartments & Residences

Santo Domingo keeps consolidating as one of the region's most important real estate markets with the arrival of Marriott Executive Apartments and Residences Santo Domingo, the first project of this Marriott concept in the Caribbean.

The development will be located on Winston Churchill Avenue, one of the capital's main corporate, commercial and residential corridors. It will be developed by Constructora Vistana under Marriott Branded Residences standards.

The tower will have 30 levels, more than 100 apartments, over 200 parking spaces and approximately 2,000 square meters of amenities.

The project will offer studio, one- and two-bedroom apartments, aimed both at residents and at investors interested in properties with professional management services.

Its main amenities include a pool, gym, running track, à la carte restaurants, multi-purpose lounges, a business center and various social areas.

A new way to live and invest

Unlike a conventional residential tower, owners will have access to unit management and administration services under Marriott-associated standards, combining the privacy of an apartment with the facilities and attentions of the hotel industry.

This model is especially attractive for those seeking a property in the center of Santo Domingo, backed by an international brand and with the possibility of generating income through rental.

Branded residences grow in the Dominican Republic

The arrival of the Caribbean's first Marriott Executive Apartments confirms the growth of branded residences in the Dominican Republic.

This real estate model, consolidated in cities such as Miami, Dubai, London and Mexico City, integrates private properties with design, services, management and standards linked to recognized international brands.

Marriott's presence in this new development strengthens Santo Domingo's position as a destination for real estate investment, business tourism and medium and long-term executive stays.

With this project, Winston Churchill Avenue continues to evolve as one of the city's main axes of real estate and business development, expanding the offer of properties aimed at local and foreign buyers and investors.

Source: Marriott Executive Apartments and Residences Santo Domingo.
